## 📍 About Lokalupdate
**Lokalupdate.com** is a hyper-local community hub designed to bridge the gap between residents and their local environment. Our platform empowers users to share real-time updates, discover local events, and support small businesses within their specific city or town.

### Core Features:
* **Hyper-Local News:** Stay informed on neighborhood developments.
* **Business Promotion:** A platform for local services to reach their target audience.
* **Community Engagement:** Post about job openings, festivals, and local activities.
* **Local Marketplace:** Buy and sell items within your community.
